Meend Pad

The board is one sitar string. The open string is tuned to mandra Ma at the far left. Pitch rises to the right, fret by fret, up to taar Pa.
  • On a phone or tablet, play with a finger or a stylus. Hold a note with one finger and tap Echo (bottom right) with another for an echo. Turn the phone sideways for more room. The Settings button at the top shows or hides the settings.
  • Press down with the pen, or click and drag with a mouse, to sound the note. The tone lasts as long as you hold it, and stops when you lift.
  • The Volume slider sets the base level. With a Wacom pen, lighter or heavier pressure moves the level around that base.
  • Left and right move along the string. Snap holds the frets of the chosen thaat like a sitar. Free slides continuously. Thin grey lines mark the notes the thaat leaves out. Fret spacing can be Equal or Geometric (closer together higher up, as on a real string).
  • The light band across the middle is the baseline. On the band you hear the fret note. Moving up from it raises the pitch, like pulling a string (meend). Moving down lowers it by the same scale, so the height of a curve always reads as pitch. Short coloured ticks on each fret mark where each swara lies, above or below that fret's note.
  • The pull can go as far as the board's edge. Faint lines mark each semitone. Depth sets how far the pen travels for the first semitone of meend. With Geometric spacing each further semitone takes a little less travel, like the frets. With Equal every semitone takes the same travel. Either way the vertical scale is the same on every fret.
  • Space or a pen side button makes an echo. The note you are holding stays sounding where it is and fades out over the Echo trail time. Nothing new starts until you lift the pen and touch down again, so you can move to the next note and start it gracefully over the trailing one. It sounds like two people, one following the other. A shrinking ring marks each echo. Set the trail slider fully right for hold. Then the left note sustains until you press Space again with no note playing. You can change the Tone while an echo sounds. The echo keeps its own instrument. X or the eraser end clears the trails and stops any echoes still sounding. T toggles the tanpura. F goes fullscreen. 1 to 5 pick the tone (Cello, Violin, Voice male, Voice female, Flute), even while you play.
Shruti tuning places each swara where Hindustani practice puts it for the chosen thaat, for example a lower komal re in Bhairav and Todi. Equal tuning uses Western equal temperament for comparison.
