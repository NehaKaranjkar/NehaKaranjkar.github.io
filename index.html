Neha Karanjkar

 




Assistant Professor, Computer Science and Engineering
School of Mathematics and Computer Science
Indian Institute of Technology Goa

Office: Room F10, Lab 2, Acad Block A,
IIT Goa, Farmagudi, Ponda, Goa 403401


I work on parallel simulation, performance modeling and simulation-driven design of discrete-event systems, and build open-source frameworks that put these ideas into practice. My recent work applies lessons from Parallel Discrete-Event Simulation (PDES) to the synchronization of multi-agent (LLM/AI) systems that act concurrently on shared state.

I received my M.Tech and Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Bombay, and was a post-doctoral fellow at the Robert Bosch Centre for Cyber-Physical Systems, IISc Bangalore, and a research scientist on the Ajit indigenous processor project at IIT Bombay, before joining IIT Goa in 2019.

Research Interests

  • Multi-agent synchronization: AI agents on shared state, conservative-to-optimistic execution, speculation and rollback

  • Parallel and hybrid simulation: parallel discrete-event simulation, mixed discrete-continuous simulation, simulation-based optimization

  • Computer systems performance modeling: cycle-level models of processors, accelerators and systems-on-chip

  • Simulation for digital twins and supply chains: automated model generation, learning-based surrogates (including graph neural networks), open simulation libraries
