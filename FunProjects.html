Meend Pad

Meend Pad is a small instrument that runs in your browser. It is made for learners and practitioners of Indian classical music, especially if you are learning an instrument. Created by Neha with Opus 5.5.

Play it here. It needs no install. It works best with a writing pad such as a Wacom tablet, or a stylus on a tablet or phone. A mouse works too.

You play by drawing. Moving left and right takes you along a sitar-like string whose frets are set to the chosen thaat. Moving up or down from the central band bends the pitch, like a meend on a string, and pen pressure sets the volume. In the spirit of the Haken Continuum, every note can be shaped continuously, so you can practise meend, gamak and andolan and see the exact curve you played.

Features

  • Sounds: male and female voice (aakar), cello, violin and a bansuri-like flute.

  • Shruti tuning for each thaat, with Western equal temperament for comparison.

  • Tanpura drone (Pa, Sa, Sa, kharaj Sa) with adjustable speed.

  • Colour-coded pitch trails. The colour of the curve shows which swara you are sounding.

  • An echo key (Space) that leaves a note sounding while you carry on, like a second singer following the first.

Tip: with a Wacom tablet, open the page in Chrome and press F for fullscreen, so the whole tablet maps onto the playing area. Press ? on the page for help.
