A New Browser-based Lecture Engagement and Continuous Evaluation System for CS 101

CS 101 retains in-person lectures and labs for a class of about 150, and uses a lecture and lab portal I designed and built to keep every student engaged. Students log in from a phone or laptop with their institute Google account and solve short questions (MCQ, ordering, debug-it and programming) as each concept is taught. Answers are checked instantly in the browser, and a live dashboard shows how many students have understood each concept before the class moves on. Attendance and participation are recorded automatically, past lectures stay open for practice anytime, and the same system runs labs and exams with automatic grading.
